# English A1 → C1: A Practical Study Program

This program is for an adult learner who:

- is around **A1** now;
- already has an A1 English book;
- finds English difficult;
- wants a clear path to **C1**;
- needs practical English, not only grammar exercises.

You do not need to understand every word in this repository. Start with
[`START-HERE.md`](START-HERE.md). Use a translator when necessary.

## The goal

At C1, you should be able to:

- understand long speech, podcasts, films, and lectures;
- read complex articles and books;
- speak fluently without searching for every word;
- write clear, well-organized professional or academic texts;
- change your language for friendly, formal, and professional situations.

C1 does **not** mean perfect English or a native accent.

## How long will it take?

Cambridge English estimates about **700–800 guided learning hours from complete
beginner to C1**. Because you are around A1, a reasonable personal target is
approximately **650–900 focused hours**, depending on your starting point,
consistency, opportunities to speak, and previous language-learning experience.

| Route | Weekly study | Approximate time |
|---|---:|---:|
| Gentle | 6–8 hours | 24–36 months |
| Standard (recommended) | 10–14 hours | 15–24 months |
| Intensive | 18–22 hours | 10–15 months |

These are estimates, not promises. Move forward when you pass the level checks,
not when a date arrives.

## Program map

| Phase | Suggested time | Main result |
|---|---:|---|
| Foundation: strong A1 → A2 | 12–16 weeks | Handle simple daily situations |
| Independence: A2 → B1 | 20–24 weeks | Manage travel, routine conversation, simple media |
| Fluency: B1 → B2 | 24–32 weeks | Discuss ideas and work with authentic content |
| Advanced: B2 → C1 | 32–40 weeks | Communicate flexibly and understand complex English |

The standard route is about **88–112 weeks**. Repeat or extend any phase when
needed.

## Five rules

1. **Study six days, rest one day.** Consistency beats rare long sessions.
2. **Use all four skills every week:** listening, speaking, reading, and writing.
3. **Review old material.** New lessons alone do not create memory.
4. **Create English.** Speak and write; do not only watch and read.
5. **Make mistakes.** Correct important repeated mistakes, then continue.

## Minimum day

When you are tired, do this 20-minute version:

1. Review words for 5 minutes.
2. Listen to easy English for 5 minutes.
3. Read aloud or shadow for 5 minutes.
4. Say or write five sentences for 5 minutes.

Never miss twice.

## Important note

This is a self-study framework, not an official course or a guarantee of a CEFR
certificate. If you need certified C1 for work, university, or immigration,
choose the required exam and add exam-specific preparation during the final
8–12 weeks.
