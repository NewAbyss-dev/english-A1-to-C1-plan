# Resources

Use a small set consistently. The method matters more than collecting links.
Most resources below have free material; some also sell optional services.

## Core free resources

### Structured skill practice

- [British Council LearnEnglish](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/) —
  grammar, vocabulary, listening, reading, writing, speaking, and video by CEFR
  level.
- [BBC Learning English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/) — courses,
  pronunciation, vocabulary, news, and short programs.
- [VOA Learning English](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/) — slower news and
  level-based audio useful from A2/B1.

### Dictionaries and examples

- [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/) — learner
  definitions, UK/US audio, grammar, and examples.
- [Oxford Learner's Dictionaries](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/)
  — level-aware definitions, pronunciation, and collocations.
- [YouGlish](https://youglish.com/) — hear words and phrases in real video
  contexts. Check several examples.

### Vocabulary review

- [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) — flexible spaced-repetition flashcards.
  Create a small personal deck instead of downloading a huge deck.

### Level descriptions and time estimates

- [Official CEFR self-assessment grid](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/table-2-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-self-assessment-grid)
- [Cambridge guided learning hours](https://support.cambridgeenglish.org/hc/en-gb/articles/202838506-Guided-learning-hours)

## What to use at each phase

### A1 → A2

Use:

- your A1 book and its audio as the main sequence;
- [A1–A2 grammar](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2);
- [A1–A2 vocabulary](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/vocabulary/a1-a2);
- [Starting Out videos](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/general/video-series/starting-out);
- a beginner graded reader with audio if possible.

### A2 → B1

Add:

- a structured B1 course or course book;
- graded readers around your level;
- VOA or BBC learner programs;
- weekly conversation practice;
- short interest-based articles and videos.

### B1 → B2

Add:

- [B1–B2 grammar](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2);
- authentic podcasts with transcripts;
- news analysis and magazines;
- contemporary fiction or accessible nonfiction;
- regular tutor/exchange feedback.

### B2 → C1

Use mostly authentic:

- long-form journalism, essays, novels, and professional texts;
- lectures, documentaries, debates, and unscripted podcasts;
- sources representing several varieties of English;
- an advanced learner's dictionary and collocations dictionary;
- official exam resources if certification is required.

## How to choose material

Keep a resource when:

- it matches your current level or is only slightly difficult;
- you know exactly how to use it each week;
- it gives transcripts, examples, answer keys, or feedback;
- its topics keep you interested;
- you actually return to it.

Remove a resource when:

- it produces passive watching without recall or output;
- it is so hard that every sentence requires translation;
- its explanations create more confusion;
- you have not used it for four weeks;
- it duplicates your main course.

## Graded readers

Choose a book labeled by CEFR level or headword count. Read for enjoyment:

1. read a chapter without stopping often;
2. guess unknown words;
3. check only words important to the story;
4. record a 1–2 minute chapter summary;
5. finish the book;
6. gradually choose harder books.

Extensive reading works through volume. One finished easy book is more useful
than ten abandoned difficult books.

## Speaking partners and tutors

When evaluating any platform or teacher, look for:

- reliable scheduling and clear safety/privacy rules;
- partners or teachers who let you speak most of the time;
- corrections after you finish your idea;
- notes on repeated patterns;
- tasks matched to your current phase.

No single commercial platform is required for this program.
