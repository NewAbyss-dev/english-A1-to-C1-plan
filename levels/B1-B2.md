# Phase 3: B1 → B2

**Length:** 24–32 weeks  
**Work:** 220–300 hours  
**Main course:** B2 course plus increasing authentic input

## Exit abilities

By the end, you can usually:

- understand extended speech and most standard-language media;
- interact with reasonable fluency and spontaneity;
- explain a viewpoint and compare advantages and disadvantages;
- read articles and contemporary prose with growing independence;
- write clear, detailed texts of about 220–280 words;
- notice and correct many of your own repeated errors.

## Block 1 — Range and accuracy (weeks 1–6)

Language:

- full tense review and tense choice in discourse;
- modal verbs in past and present;
- passive forms and causatives;
- articles, determiners, and quantifiers;
- complex noun phrases and relative clauses.

Practice:

- build an error log from old writing and recordings;
- transform simple sentences into precise connected ones;
- summarize one 8–15 minute podcast each week;
- write one formal and one informal text.

## Block 2 — Natural vocabulary (weeks 7–12)

Focus:

- collocations and dependent prepositions;
- phrasal verbs in context;
- word families and affixes;
- connotation, register, and common idiomatic language;
- chunks for agreeing, interrupting, clarifying, and hedging.

Practice:

- collect phrases from authentic sources, not long word lists;
- compare dictionary examples;
- use each selected phrase in speech and writing;
- paraphrase when the exact word is unavailable.

Project:

> Record a 10-minute conversation or monologue. Transcribe two minutes, identify
> unnatural wording, and record an improved version.

## Block 3 — Argument and structure (weeks 13–18)

Language:

- second, third, and mixed conditionals;
- reported language;
- participle clauses and advanced linking;
- emphasis and inversion for recognition;
- stance language: certainty, probability, caution, and evaluation.

Practice:

- analyze claim, evidence, counterargument, and conclusion;
- debate familiar social or professional issues;
- write an opinion, discussion, report, and review;
- revise organization before correcting sentence-level errors.

## Block 4 — Authentic independence (weeks 19–24)

Use authentic material for at least 60% of input:

- podcasts and interviews;
- news analysis and magazine articles;
- films or series with English subtitles when needed;
- a novel, accessible nonfiction book, or audiobook.

Each week:

1. choose one substantial topic;
2. read two sources and listen to one source;
3. collect 10–15 useful phrases;
4. discuss the topic;
5. write a 220–280 word synthesis or response.

## Consolidation (optional weeks 25–32)

Choose one specialization: work, study, technology, travel/social, or an exam.
Practice the tasks you will actually perform.

Examples:

- lead a simulated meeting and write the follow-up email;
- understand a lecture and produce organized notes;
- explain technical documentation to a non-specialist;
- compare sources and write a recommendation;
- complete timed official sample tasks.

Final project:

> Research one debatable question with three reliable English sources. Give a
> 10-minute presentation, answer five minutes of questions, and write a
> 250-word balanced essay after receiving feedback.

## Weekly targets

- 3–4 hours authentic listening and reading;
- two conversations totaling at least 90 minutes;
- one prepared presentation or structured discussion;
- one substantial text, followed by revision;
- two pronunciation sessions focused on connected speech;
- vocabulary review with active use;
- one enjoyable activity entirely in English.

## Plateau strategy

B1→B2 often feels slow. Progress by improving quality:

- replace vague words with precise phrases;
- support opinions with examples and evidence;
- speak in longer organized turns;
- listen to several accents;
- rewrite corrected work;
- revisit the same topic and perform it better.

## Exit check

Complete the B2 milestone in [`../ASSESSMENT.md`](../ASSESSMENT.md). A single
online multiple-choice score is not enough; B2 requires spontaneous production.
