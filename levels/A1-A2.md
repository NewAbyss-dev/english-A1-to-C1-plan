# Phase 1: Strong A1 → A2

**Length:** 12–16 weeks  
**Work:** 120–160 hours  
**Main course:** your A1 book, followed by early A2 material

## Exit abilities

By the end, you can usually:

- understand common phrases and short, clear messages;
- introduce yourself and ask/answer routine questions;
- handle simple shops, cafés, transport, appointments, and directions;
- describe family, home, work, routines, past events, and plans;
- read short notices, messages, menus, and simple stories;
- write connected notes and messages of about 80–120 words.

## Block 1 — Me and my world (weeks 1–4)

Topics:

- introductions, countries, languages, family;
- numbers, time, dates, prices;
- home, possessions, food, and daily routine.

Language:

- `be`, `have`, subject/object pronouns, possessives;
- articles, plurals, `this/that/these/those`;
- present simple, frequency adverbs;
- `there is/are`, `some/any`, countable/uncountable nouns;
- basic questions and negatives.

Projects:

1. Record a two-minute personal introduction.
2. Describe your room or home with 10–15 sentences.
3. Make a weekly timetable and explain it aloud.
4. Role-play buying food and asking the price.

## Block 2 — Daily communication (weeks 5–8)

Topics:

- work and study, hobbies, clothes, weather;
- town, transport, shopping, restaurants;
- abilities, requests, and invitations.

Language:

- present continuous and present simple contrast;
- `can/can't`, imperatives, object pronouns;
- prepositions of place and time;
- comparatives and superlatives;
- `would like`, polite requests, basic connectors.

Projects:

1. Give directions from your home to a familiar place.
2. Role-play ordering a meal and solving one small problem.
3. Compare two cities, products, or people.
4. Invite someone to an activity and arrange time/place.

## Block 3 — Past and future (weeks 9–12)

Topics:

- holidays, health, life events, plans;
- experiences, feelings, simple stories.

Language:

- past simple of common regular and irregular verbs;
- `was/were`, time expressions;
- `going to` and present continuous for plans;
- basic `will` for predictions and immediate decisions;
- adverbs of manner and sequence words.

Projects:

1. Tell a three-minute story about yesterday or a trip.
2. Write a 100-word story using `first`, `then`, `after that`, and `finally`.
3. Explain your plans for next weekend and next year.
4. Role-play making a doctor's appointment.

## Block 4 — A2 consolidation (optional weeks 13–16)

Use this block if skills are uneven or your A1 book did not cover early A2.

Add:

- present perfect for simple life experiences;
- `should`, `must`, and `have to`;
- infinitive/gerund after common verbs;
- relative clauses with `who`, `which`, and `that`;
- more listening at natural but careful speed.

Final project:

> Create “My Life Now and Next Year”: a 3–5 minute recording plus a 100–120
> word text. Include routines, one past experience, abilities, comparisons, and
> plans.

## Weekly targets

- 1–2 units or equivalent from the main course;
- 60–75 minutes of vocabulary review;
- 90 minutes of listening;
- 90–120 minutes of speaking/pronunciation;
- one short reading and one short written text;
- one practical role-play.

## Common problems

- **Translating word by word:** memorize short sentence frames such as “I usually
  ___, but today I’m ___.”
- **Silent study:** read answers aloud and record a short message every day.
- **Too many words:** prioritize 800–1,500 high-use words and personal phrases;
  exact counts are not level certificates.
- **Fear of past tense:** tell one tiny past story every day.

## Exit check

Complete the A2 milestone in [`../ASSESSMENT.md`](../ASSESSMENT.md). If one skill
is weak, spend 2–4 weeks repairing that skill before starting the full B1 plan.
